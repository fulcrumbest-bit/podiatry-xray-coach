Standard views (DP, oblique, lateral, mortise), weight-bearing vs non-weight-bearing, a systematic reading checklist, key angles (HVA, IMA, Meary, Böhler, calcaneal pitch) and the Ottawa rules for when to image.

4 · Track progress

How podiatrists use X-ray in Australia: registered podiatrists can request Medicare-rebatable plain radiographs of the foot, ankle, leg, femur and knee (MBS items 57521, 57523, 57527). Interpreting those films well is a core graduate capability — this app trains exactly that skill.
